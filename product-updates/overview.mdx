## What you'll find

Entries are grouped by release period, usually covering about a month. Each opens with the most significant features, then lists the smaller additions, improvements and fixes that went out alongside them.

Entries stay short on purpose. Where a feature has a guide, the entry links to it, so you can read the announcement and then go straight to the instructions.

## Finding a specific release

The contents panel on the right of the changelog lists every entry, so you can jump straight to a release instead of scrolling for it. Each entry has its own address — right-click a contents link to copy it, and you can send someone directly to the release you mean rather than to the top of the page.

<Tip>Looking for a change in a particular area? The changelog is one page, so your browser's find (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd>) searches the whole history at once — "pickface" or "invoice" will land you in the right entries.</Tip>

## Subscribing to updates

The changelog publishes an RSS feed at `/product-updates/changelog/rss.xml`, so new releases can reach you without checking the page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the feed address">
    Open the [changelog](/product-updates/changelog) and use the RSS button at the top of the page, or take this site's address and append `/product-updates/changelog/rss.xml`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Into Slack">
    In any Slack channel, run `/feed subscribe` followed by the feed address. New releases are then posted to that channel automatically — useful for an operations or account management channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Into email or a feed reader">
    Any RSS-to-email service works — point it at the same feed address. Feed readers take it directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's covered

The changelog covers changes you can see and act on:

- New features and settings.
- Changes to how existing screens and processes behave.
- Fixes for faults that affected customers generally.
- Performance, security and compliance changes worth knowing about.

<Info>Not every fix appears here. If something you reported only affected your own data or configuration, your Clarus contact will confirm it with you directly rather than it being listed as a product change.</Info>

<Info>Looking for how something works rather than when it changed? The [knowledgebase](/) is the reference; the changelog only tells you what moved and when.</Info>

## Telling us what to build

If something in these updates is close to what you need but not quite there, say so — most of what ships here started as a customer request. Raise it through the in-app help widget, or talk to your Clarus contact.
