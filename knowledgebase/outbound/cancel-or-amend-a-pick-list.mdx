## Cancelling or Amending a Pick List

Whether you need to add or remove products, you can cancel a pick list or change its status to make adjustments. If only one order on a pick list cannot be shipped, you can [cancel its pack task](#cancelling-a-single-pack-task) instead, leaving the rest of the pick list untouched.

## Cancelling a Pick List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the pick lists">
    On the left-hand menu, click **Goods Out** then **Picking Lists**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the pick list to cancel">
    Find and click on the pick list you want to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the pick list">
    At the top of the screen, click the **Cancel** button. The status will change to **Cancelled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the sales order">
    Go back to the linked sales order. Its status will revert to **Pending**, allowing you to add or remove products.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Amending a Completed Pick List

If the pick list has already been completed, you can still make changes by adjusting its status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Unconfirm dispatch tasks">
    Select the pick list and go to the **Dispatch** tab. Unconfirm each product line by clicking the **Confirmed** button, or use **Unconfirm All Dispatch Tasks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unconfirm pick tasks">
    Switch to the **Pick** tab and unconfirm all product lines in the same way. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amend the sales order">
    The pick list status will change back to **Picking**. You can now return to the sales order and make amendments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>Cancelling a pick list does not affect stock levels unless the products have already been dispatched. Once cancelled, you will need to create a new pick list to proceed with the order.</Info>

## Cancelling a Single Pack Task

When one order on a pick list cannot be shipped — the item is damaged during packing, or the customer cancels before dispatch — you can cancel just that order's pack task. This is built for [consolidated picks](/knowledgebase/outbound/consolidated-picking), where many orders share one completed pick task and unconfirming the pick would mean reversing every order on it.

Cancelling a pack task:

- Returns that unit's stock to **Available**, on its current storage unit and location. The stock is not moved automatically.
- Resets the sales order to **Pending**. An order with other lines still on the pick list goes to **Part**.
- Records an unpick and an unallocate transaction for the stock, as cancelling a pick task does.
- Leaves the completed pick task, its picking charges and every other order on the pick list exactly as they were.

<Warning>Cancelling a pack task is permanent. The task is shown as **Cancelled** and cannot be reinstated. To fulfil the order again, reprocess picks from the sales order to create a new pick list.</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pick list">
    On the left-hand menu, click **Goods Out** then **Picking Lists**, and open the pick list that holds the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the pack task">
    Go to the **Pack** tab and find the row for the order you want to take back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the pack task">
    Click **Cancel** on that row. A confirmation dialog explains that the action cannot be undone, that the stock returns to available and that the sales order is reset to pending. Click **Cancel pack task** to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deal with the stock">
    The stock stays where it is, often in the goods out area. Move it back into storage with a transfer, or record a stock adjustment if it is damaged — any reason code is captured on the adjustment, not when you cancel the pack task.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If every remaining pack and dispatch task on the pick list is complete after you cancel, the pick list is marked **Complete**.

<Info>You can only cancel a pack task that has not yet been completed. Cancelling is blocked if part of the order has already been packed, if the stock is already assigned to a shipment, or if other stock has already been packed into the same storage unit — unpack first, then cancel.</Info>

<Note>The **Cancel** button only appears to users whose role includes the **Cancel Pack Tasks** ability. It is available on the pick list screen, not on the packing desk.</Note>

<Warning>If the cancelled unit is on a dispatch container, transfer it off before dispatching the container. A dispatch container that still holds available stock cannot be dispatched.</Warning>

The same action works on a regular, non-consolidated pick list, but there it is usually simpler to cancel or amend the pick list as described above.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I unconfirm a pick task after cancelling a pack task?">
    Once a pack task generated from a pick task has been cancelled, that pick task can no longer be unconfirmed. Cancelling is permanent, so reprocess the order from the sales order to fulfil it again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
