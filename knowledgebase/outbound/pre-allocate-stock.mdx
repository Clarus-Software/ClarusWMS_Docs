Pre-allocation lets you hand-pick available stock and hold it for a sales order before the order is sent to pick. Held stock moves to a **Pre-Allocated** status, so no other order can allocate or pick it, and when you process picks the order is picked from exactly the stock you reserved.

Use it when you need to guarantee stock for an order that will be picked later, for example a key customer's delivery, while other orders keep flowing through the warehouse.

<Info>
Pre-allocation is being rolled out gradually. If you can't see the **Pre-Allocate** button on your sales order lines, contact Clarus support.
</Info>

## Before you start

- You need the **Pre-Allocations** ability with **Manage** access to pre-allocate, edit or release stock. With **Read** access only, you can see pre-allocations but the buttons are disabled. See [Permissions](#permissions).
- The sales order must be saved and have a status of **Pending**, **Pre-Allocated** or **Part Pre-Allocated**.

## Pre-allocate stock to an order line

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sales order">
    Open the sales order from the **Sales Orders** data grid. If it's a new order, save it first — the **Pre-Allocate** button is disabled until the order is saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Pre-Allocate on the line">
    In the order lines, click **Pre-Allocate** on the line you want to reserve stock for. The **Pre-Allocate Stock** window opens, showing the product and a summary such as *0 of 10 pre-allocated, 10 remaining*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the quantities to hold">
    The **Available Stock** grid lists the stock you can choose from. Enter a quantity in the **Quantity To Pre-Allocate** column against each stock record you want to hold. You can:

    - Spread the line across several stock records.
    - Take part of a stock record — the rest stays available. For example, if a pallet holds 20 and the line needs 5, pre-allocate 5 and the other 15 remain available to other orders.
    - Enter decimal quantities.
    - Pre-allocate less than the ordered quantity. The rest of the line is allocated as normal when you process picks.

    The summary updates as you type to show how much you've selected against what remains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm Pre-Allocation**. The stock is held for the order line and the order's status updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Which stock you can choose

- Only stock with a status of **Available** in the order's warehouse is shown.
- Stock in a pickface location can't be pre-allocated.
- Batch, sell-by date and storage unit rules are **not** applied — you're choosing the stock manually, so you can pick any available stock record for the product.
- If the order's account is set up to order from linked accounts, stock from those linked accounts is also shown. When you pre-allocate linked-account stock, it's transferred into the order's account with a **LINKED ACCOUNT TRANSFER** reason code.
- The total pre-allocated on a line can't exceed the line's ordered quantity.

## What happens when stock is pre-allocated

| Where | What you'll see |
|-------|-----------------|
| Sales order status | **Pre-Allocated** when every line is fully covered, **Part Pre-Allocated** when only some of the order is. |
| Sales order page | A **Pre-Allocated Stock** grid listing each held stock record, its quantity and location. |
| Sales Orders data grid | A **Pre-Allocated** column showing the total pre-allocated against the total ordered across all lines, for example 5/10. |
| Stocks data grid | The held stock shows a status of **Pre-Allocated**. Add the **Pre-Allocated Sales Order Line** column to see which order it's held for. |
| [Stock Balance](/knowledgebase/products-and-stock/stock-balance) | The quantity moves from **Available** into its own **Pre-Allocated** balance, separate from **Allocated**. The ordered balance reduces by the same amount. |
| Transactions | A **Pre-Allocated** transaction is recorded for each stock record held. |
| Audit log | The order's status change is logged with who made it and when. |

If you hold only part of a stock record, it's split in two: a **Pre-Allocated** record for the quantity you held and an **Available** record for the remainder. Reducing a held quantity later works the same way in reverse.

## Edit or release pre-allocated stock

While the order is **Pre-Allocated** or **Part Pre-Allocated**, you can change what's held from the **Pre-Allocated Stock** grid on the sales order page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Reduce the quantity">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enter the new quantity">
        In the **Quantity** column, type the quantity you want to keep holding. It must be greater than zero and less than the quantity currently held.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Click **Save** on that row. The difference is released back to **Available** straight away.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Release a stock record">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Release">
        Click **Release** on the row for the stock record you no longer need.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Confirm the release. The stock is no longer held for this order and becomes available to other orders immediately.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Add more stock">
    If a line is only partly pre-allocated, click **Pre-Allocate** on the line again and select more stock, as described in [Pre-allocate stock to an order line](#pre-allocate-stock-to-an-order-line). You can add up to the quantity still remaining on the line.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Each change updates the order's status and balances, and records an **Un-Pre-Allocated** transaction for the released quantity:

- Some stock still held → **Part Pre-Allocated**
- Every line still fully covered → stays **Pre-Allocated**
- All pre-allocated stock released → back to **Pending**

<Note>
Once the order has been processed for picking, the **Pre-Allocated Stock** grid becomes read-only and its pre-allocation can no longer be changed.
</Note>

## Changing order lines with pre-allocated stock

To protect the stock you've held, some line changes are blocked until you release the pre-allocation first:

- You can't reduce a line's order quantity below its pre-allocated quantity. Reduce or release the pre-allocation, then change the line.
- You can't change the product on a line that has pre-allocated stock.
- You can't delete a line that has pre-allocated stock.

## Processing picks on a pre-allocated order

When you [process picks](/knowledgebase/outbound/create-and-process-a-pick-list) on a **Pre-Allocated** or **Part Pre-Allocated** order:

- Pick tasks are created from exactly the stock you pre-allocated — the system doesn't look for different stock.
- Any quantity on a line that wasn't pre-allocated is matched from available stock using your normal pick rules.
- The quantity moves out of the **Pre-Allocated** balance as the stock is allocated to the pick.

## Cancelling orders, pick lists and pick tasks

Cancelling releases pre-allocated stock automatically, returning it to **Available**:

- **Cancelling a sales order** that is **Pre-Allocated** or **Part Pre-Allocated** releases all of its pre-allocated stock.
- **Cancelling a pick list**, or an individual pick task, for a pre-allocated order returns that stock to **Available**. It's no longer held for the order, so pre-allocate again if you still need to reserve it. See [Cancel or amend a pick list](/knowledgebase/outbound/cancel-or-amend-a-pick-list).

## Stock control on pre-allocated stock

While stock is **Pre-Allocated**, the following operations are blocked:

- Positive and negative adjustments
- Account transfers
- Warehouse transfers
- Hold and release from hold
- Moves

If you try one of these, you're told the stock is pre-allocated to a sales order. Release the pre-allocation from the sales order first, then carry out the operation.

## Permissions

Pre-allocation has its own **Pre-Allocations** ability, set on the user's role. See [Creating and implementing user roles](/knowledgebase/users-and-roles/user-roles-guide).

| Access | What the user can do |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Read** | See the **Pre-Allocated** count on the Sales Orders data grid and the **Pre-Allocated Stock** grid on the sales order page. The **Pre-Allocate**, **Save** and **Release** buttons are shown but disabled. |
| **Manage** | Pre-allocate, edit and release stock. Manage also requires Read. |

The Admin role has both **Read** and **Manage** by default. Other roles need the ability added.

This ability only controls pre-allocation on sales orders. Users still see the **Pre-Allocated** stock status and balance through their existing Stocks and Stock Balance access.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why is the Pre-Allocate button disabled?">
Check that the order is saved and has a status of **Pending**, **Pre-Allocated** or **Part Pre-Allocated**, that the line isn't already fully pre-allocated, and that your role has **Manage** access to **Pre-Allocations**. Hover over the button to see the reason.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why can't I see some of my stock in the Pre-Allocate Stock window?">
Only **Available** stock in the order's warehouse is listed. Stock that's held, allocated, already pre-allocated, or in a pickface location isn't shown.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I pre-allocate serial-numbered, kitted or cross-dock orders?">
No. Pre-allocation doesn't support serial number selection, kitted products or cross-dock flows.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do automations still run when I pre-allocate?">
Yes. Pre-allocating, editing and releasing stock trigger the existing sales order updated and stock balances updated automations.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
