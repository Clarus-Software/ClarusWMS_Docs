## What Is a Charge Window?

A charge window sets when an account's billing period closes. Each day you select is a day the window **closes**. When it closes, the account's open charges are closed off for invoicing and a new period starts.

You assign a charge window to an account alongside its charge card — see [Set charges to accounts](/knowledgebase/financial/set-charges-to-accounts).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open charge windows">
    From the left-hand menu, click **Charges** then **Charge Windows**. This displays all existing charge windows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new charge window">
    Click the add button at the bottom of the grid to open **New Charge Window**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter charge window details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    - **Name**: Give the charge window a clear name (e.g., "Month End").
    - **Frequency**: Choose **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
      - **Weekly**: Under **Select day(s)**, pick the weekdays the window closes on. Select every day to close the window daily.
      - **Monthly**: Under **Select day(s)**, pick the days of the month the window closes on, from 1 to 31.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the charge window">
    Click **Save** to create the charge window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Monthly windows in shorter months

If you choose the 29th, 30th or 31st for a monthly window, the window still closes in months that don't have that day. It closes on the **last calendar day of that month** instead.

| Day selected | February (28 days) | February (leap year) | April, June, September, November |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29th | 28th | 29th | 29th |
| 30th | 28th | 29th | 30th |
| 31st | 28th | 29th | 30th |

For example, a window set to close on the 31st closes on 31 January, 28 February, 31 March and 30 April. Each month's charges stay in that month's billing period.

When you select the 29th, 30th or 31st, the charge window screen shows a message as a reminder of this behaviour.

<Tip>To bill on the last day of every month, select the **31st**. The window then closes on the final day of each month, whatever its length.</Tip>

<Note>Days are not combined. If you select both the 30th and the 31st, the window closes once on 30 April — not twice.</Note>

## What happens when a window closes

The window's closing day drives several charges:

- The account's open charges are closed off for invoicing, and a new billing period starts.
- [Recurring charges](/knowledgebase/financial/recurring-charges) with the **End of Charge Window** frequency are added.
- [Step-priced](/knowledgebase/financial/step-pricing) rental charges using flat tiers are calculated for the whole window.

<Tip>After creating the charge window, your next step is to set up a **Charge Card** that defines the actual charges, then assign both to the account.</Tip>
